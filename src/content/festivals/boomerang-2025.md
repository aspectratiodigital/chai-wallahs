Expect turntable mastery, euphoric live bands, and DJs who know exactly when to turn up the heat. Whether it's pulsing club sounds, festival anthems, or deep, hypnotic rhythms, Boomerang is where music and movement come together in all their glory.

Boomerang Festival hands the reins over to a selection of boundary-pushing promoters and artists, each curating their own sonic universe. From deep, pulsating house beats under the trees to kaleidoscopic live acts and experiential performances, every corner of the festival is an unpredictable and pleasurable sensory overload. Where will you find yourself?

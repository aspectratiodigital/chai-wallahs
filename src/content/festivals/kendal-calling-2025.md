## Explore

Escape to the Lakes and stumble upon a whole new world. For one weekend a year, we transform this ancient Deer Park into a feast for the senses — a picturesque wonderland built for exploration.

## Parklands

Step inside the upside-down garden and experience something other-worldly. There is so much to unpack here — from those throwback anthems you love to hear from household names, to the biggest and boldest dance music around, Parklands is home to nothing but good vibes and great moments.

## Lost Eden

The jewel in Kendal Calling's crown, Lost Eden is an area with creativity and escapism at its core. Never the same two years in a row, it's all about the magic and wonder of discovery, trying something new and allowing yourself to be immersed in the arts, culture and awe of the mesmerising installations, with a multitude of micro venues and entertainment galore.

## Desert Island Disco

Ever since Nile Rodgers and CHIC in 2019, disco has had a home at Kendal Calling. Expect nothing but the finest selectors, spinning disco, motown, soul and funk from midday to the early hours — from a cocktail on a sunny afternoon to pulling your finest moves at 2am, one thing is certain: your feet won't stop moving.

The earliest event in Chai Wallahs' recorded Facebook history. Launch of a long-term collaboration between Chai Wallahs, Diplomats of Sound, Stone'd Records and Drop4Drop to raise awareness of the world's water crisis, aiming to raise £50,000 through ticket sales and an auction of Thai artist Pakpoom Silaphan's artwork. Lineup: The Boxettes, Yes Sir Boss, Dizraeli and Parker.

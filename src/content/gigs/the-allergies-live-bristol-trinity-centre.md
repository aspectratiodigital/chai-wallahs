The Allergies are back, bigger than ever, with a night of funk, soul and hip hop at Trinity Centre. Their brand of feel-good funk and hyped-up hip-hop has been delighting dance floors since 2016. Joining them on stage is LA rap legend Andy Cooper, soul sensation Marietta Smith, and sax supremo James Morton. Warming up the turntables is Boca 45.

Chai Wallahs & Snuffy Jacks presents: Chai Roots Acoustic. An evening of intimate acoustic music, a celebration of song, lyricism, and harmony. Edition 1 features exclusive performances from Bristol's very own Hibiscus, and Jake Blackmore AKA MC Binbags. Hosted by Chai Wallahs all rounder and long term advocate of grassroots musicians and independent venues, Potter. This promises to be a very special evening indeed. Free entry, donations collected for musicians on the night.

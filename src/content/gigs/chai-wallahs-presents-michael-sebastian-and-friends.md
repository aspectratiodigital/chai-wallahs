Michael Sebastian creates Live Dance Music. It has the structure of electronic; the freedom of jazz, and the depth of world. An improvisatory joy, a muscular blast of indie-electronics worth filing alongside Bonobo or Bicep - Clash. A South African in London, Sebastian has a strong presence in the city with recent headline shows at EartH Hackney & Colour Factory, support slots for James Blake and Four Tet at Alexandra Palace, as well as being named a top 5 global Sofar artist.

There's a full line-up of live music, so why not transform your Green Man weekend into a week-long Welsh adventure, with plenty of time to soak up the secrets of the Usk Valley before the weekend festivities begin. Roam ancient forests, follow the trail of a river into the unknown, and climb the lofty peaks that watch over you once the festival unfurls.

## Ten Bars

When you're in need of a little tipple to wash it all down with, there's ten bars, all open late, to choose from. Wet your whistle with a choice a plenty — whether that's beers, ales, cider, wine, cocktails or a non-boozy beverage.

## Family Camping Fields

After a more slow-paced camping experience to nestle down in with your brood? Our Family Camping fields offer exactly that, with nappy bins and a chilled vibe making the perfect place for your family to call home over the weekend.

You don't need a special ticket to camp here — you just turn up and pitch your tent. No single or groups of adults will be permitted in the Family Campsite unless accompanied by children 16 or under.

The Settler's Pass is your ticket to one whole week in Green Man land. Settle in, relax, explore.

Let the mighty gates roll open to welcome the Settlers. From Monday to Wednesday before the main weekend, a miscellany of activities and workshops await at the Settlement: from misty mountain morning yoga to toasted marshmallows by moonlight, from communal BBQs to the Settlement pub quiz, from foraging to fire shows and silent discos under the stars. There's a full line-up of live music too, so why not transform your Green Man weekend into a week-long Welsh adventure, with plenty of time to soak up the secrets of the Usk Valley before the weekend festivities begin. Roam ancient forests, follow the trail of a river into the unknown, and climb the lofty peaks that watch over you once the festival unfurls.

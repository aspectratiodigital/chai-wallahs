Featuring an array of special guests and one-off surprises, this is a performance not to be missed in the utterly perfect surroundings of The Jam Jar.

JAMU amalgamates an array of sample influences. By seamlessly blending cuts and scratches of sounds from around the world, the group creates an electrifying ambiance that encapsulates the essence of hip hop while transcending borders. What sets JAMU apart is their electrifying live performances, featuring not only MCs delivering thought-provoking lyricism but also the vibrant energy of a live horn section. This unique blend of traditional hip hop elements with live instrumentation elevates their shows to an unforgettable and immersive experience for fans.

Their debut album, *Hear The Sound*, promises to define their sonic character with its eclectic sound. Diplomats of Sound DJs fill in the quiet spaces with utter bangers till bedtime.

*Entry requirements: 14+, any under 18s accompanied by a 21+ adult on a 1:1 ratio*

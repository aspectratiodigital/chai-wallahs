**She's Got Brass — 9:30pm · VooKoo DJs — 10:45pm · Diplomats of Sound DJs — Midnight–1am**

A packed bill sees us welcome She's Got Brass to Bristol for their debut headline show, and with VooKoo & Diplomats of Sound DJs providing an undulating backdrop of utter bangers, all in the sumptuous surroundings of the really quite delectable Southbank Club (BS3 1DB), this promises to kick off the festive season in style.

## She's Got Brass

London's groundbreaking 10-piece female-fronted brass band. With a dynamic rhythm section featuring percussion and kit alongside the rich tones of tuba and baritone saxophone, the ensemble boasts renowned female players from the heart of the London Jazz scene — from acts like Seed Ensemble, Colectiva, New Regency Orchestra, Bonita House Band and TC and the Groove Family. They infuse their performances with a fusion of original compositions and beloved funk and soul classics, all with a contemporary twist.

## VooKoo DJs

A dynamic crew dedicated to spreading the vibrant sounds of AfroFunk, Highlife, Latin Grooves, Hip-Hop, Disco & more. With a passion for blending an eclectic array of feel-good rhythm-centric genres, their electrifying sets take listeners on a journey across continents and eras.

## Diplomats of Sound DJs

Diving in and out of more genres than you can shake a stick at, this society of sensational selectors provide an undulating, eclectic backdrop of powerful tunes, hand-picked, twisted up and sent directly to your mind tank. Expect grooves for any get-down, tunes for any tango, bangers for any boogie.

*Entry requirements: 18+*

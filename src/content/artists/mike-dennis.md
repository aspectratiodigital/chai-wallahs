This is the debut project from Jonathan Enser (Nubiyan Twist), a multi-instrumentalist and composer. He has assembled an ensemble featuring some of the most powerful forces exploring music in the UK, each bringing a unique voice to his compositions. Musicians from Nerija, Nala Sinephro, Nubya Garcia, Werkha, and beyond contribute to ‘MATTERS UNKNOWN,' a project that commands attention.

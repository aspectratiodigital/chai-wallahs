Michael Sebastian collects his favourite musicians & dancers to create a wild show of improvisational joy. Sebastian improvises dance music. His music has the structure of electronic; the freedom of jazz, and the depth of world music. A renowned live artist & dance musician, London busker, Sofar Sounds headliner, this show sees Sebastian collate all of his worlds for an epic party.

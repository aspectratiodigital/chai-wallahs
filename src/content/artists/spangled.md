Manchester born Post Punk Indie band Spangled have spent their infancy as a band honing their stagecraft and songwriting, racking up over 1 million plays world wide. Known for their unmatched energy on stage, the four piece are starting a new chapter with recent addition Joe Kilroy on drums.

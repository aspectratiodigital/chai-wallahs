Big Band of Boom is Birmingham's rock juggernaut. This 12-piece behemoth is set to re-define everything you know about heavy guitars and big-band brass! Known for an electrifying live show, BBOB have played at festival stages across the UK and Europe including Boomtown, Northside, Rudolstadt and Camp Bestival as well as hosting and programming their own stage at Shambala Festival.

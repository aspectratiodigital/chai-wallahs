Sound of the Sirens are an acoustic singer-songwriting duo based in Exeter, Devon comprising of Abbe Wood & Hannah Walker. Honing their craft over the past decade, Wood & Walker have cemented themselves as firm favourites not only on the Westcountry scene, but nationally, and internationally too.

2023 saw the life long friends and americana, roots rock n roll duo from Bristol take their songs further afield than ever before. Armed with their crowd funded debut album ‘Heart of the Matter' and latest release ‘Live from White Noise; 'they toured the UK, supported Lukas Nelson & the Promise of the Real.​

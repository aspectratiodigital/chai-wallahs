Bristol-based Simaly Sound are an all-female Dub/Reggae collective of singers, DJs and instrumentalists, who are hitting the ground running with close-knit harmonies and killer tunes. Formed in 2022 to combine the immense skill and drive of each member. Simaly Sound features members from Italy, Spain and Poland

British Born, Sweden based, Nigerian Story Teller Joshua Idehen is a true wordsmith. Silver tongued, Rich Tones, Gospel, Feel Good Choruses and Whompy beats to lift the spirit, move the body and transport the mind. Before embarking on his solo career in collaboration with Producer Ludvig Parment; JI contributed Poetry to the work of Mercury Nominees ‘The Comet is Coming’ and ‘Son’s of Kemet’.

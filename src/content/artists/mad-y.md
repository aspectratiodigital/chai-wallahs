MĀDŁY is a Parisian-born, Bristol based singer, MC and songwriter. During lockdown, she has been collaborating with different worldwide producers, to deliver her debut EP ‘The End of Me' released in May 2021. Expect some Neo-Soul with a pinch of Hip-Hop, a sprinkle of Nu-Jazz, French lyricism.

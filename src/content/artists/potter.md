Potts is a natural soul singer, lyrically honest with a penchant for a catchy chorus, He's as happy on his own with a guitar and kick drum as he is fronting a heavy band. having begun writing and recording his debut solo project, armed with a bunch of new songs, Potts is back doing what he loves most, Performing live to the people. Expect carefully crafted songs, unforgettable riffs and virtuosic guitar playing all wrapped up in a soaring tenner vocal.

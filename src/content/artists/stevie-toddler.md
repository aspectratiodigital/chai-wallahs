Progressive-pop perfection somewhere between St Vincent, Feist and Hiatus Kaiyote, Stevie Toddler's grooving bass playing and unforgettable melodies float her gracefully to the top echelons of Bristol's scene. Her 2 albums received wide spread attention, including plays from Guy Garvey (BBC Radio 6) and Jamie Cullum (BBC Radio 2).

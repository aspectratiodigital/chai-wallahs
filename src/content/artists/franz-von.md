The inimitable Franz Von. You may recognise Franz as the high octane emcee of much loved afrofusion kings K.O.G. & The Zongo Brigade, and jazz fusionists TC & The Groove Family. In 2024 he brings his own unique flavour of Afro, Hiphop and Jazz to the stage with his brand new solo project, featuring an all star, razor sharp band, synonymous with the integrity, passion, and virtuosic musicianship you'd expect from one of Diplomats of Sounds newest collaborators.

Crash-landing between funk and punk, Flat Moon are a bizarre 6-piece collective that create and perform high-energy groove-centred music. Flat Moon are known for their electrifying live shows that induce their audiences into a state of genre-fluid delirium. Their music explores themes of escapism through a neuro divergent lens, while prioritising a fun and chaotic experience.

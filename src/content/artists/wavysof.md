It's still early days for this Essex based, self taught, 21 year old producer, singer, rapper, songwriter and multi-instrumentalist ...but there's been a rich and varied life lived in Wavysof's short years. Every tricky situation and circumstance she’s found encompassed in her societal troubles are displayed within her undeniable story-telling ability, underground melodic sounds and the incredible honesty and relatability within her lyrics.

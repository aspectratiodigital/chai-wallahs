Will Varley is a singer songwriter from Deal on the UK's Kent coast, He is influenced by the great American and Canadian songwriters of the 1960's and 70's including Bob Dylan, Neil Young, Bruce Springsteen and Tracy Chapman. He has gone from selling 60 tickets to his first album launch in 2014 to headlining London's 2000 capacity Shepherd's Bush Empire in 2018.

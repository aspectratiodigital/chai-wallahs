Conn3ct - a project born out of a desire to connect souls, connect musical heritage, connect powerful female spirit, connect audiences, and collectively share the energy that each of these unique performers bring in one monumental 90-minute show featuring Bristol's MĀDŁY, London's Amy True, and Sheffield's very own Aziza Jaye. See the incredible Conn3ct House band transform live on stage from a Hip-Hop band to a Neo Soul band, to an Afro-Jazz band as we take you on a musical journey.

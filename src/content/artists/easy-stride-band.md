Easy Stride Band use a deft fusion of soul and reggae to create upbeat, feel-good music, combining live vocal and guitar loops with deep bass and head-nodding beats.  Fun, fresh and fantastic! Immediately captivating, this uplifting trio scream positivity and celebration, a sentiment which is echoed through the band's performance style.

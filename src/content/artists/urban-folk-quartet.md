The award-winning UFQ have ignited international audiences with their high-energy, genre-defying acoustic music since 2009. Joe Broughton, Paloma Trigas, Dan Walsh and Tom Chapman are the Urban Folk Quartet. Four highly accomplished musicians, a dozen instruments and four voices coming together to craft a knockout show of globally-influenced, electrifying acoustic music that has been taking the European folk scene by storm.

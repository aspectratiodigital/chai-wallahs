MCS Binbags and Dur Brain are joined on stage by DMC finalist, DJ Mr Fitz. This is a return to the roots of what make Binbag Wisdom great: Two best friends, sharing their infectious humour through deft lyricism on hard hitting beats. With their 4th studio release on the way soon, the boys are very much ‘back in town'. The undeniably charming stage chemistry of Binbag Wisdom is backed by their incredible skill.
